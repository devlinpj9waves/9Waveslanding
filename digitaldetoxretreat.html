The 9 Waves Experience

Fire & Ice in the Cyprus Hills

Two full contrast therapy sessions led by Paul. A physical reset to match the digital one.

Monday Afternoon

Introduction to Hot & Cold Therapy

Your first session eases you in with the science and practice of contrast therapy. By the end you'll wonder why you ever needed a phone to feel alive.

  • Introduction to the benefits of hot and cold therapy
  • Breathing techniques and preparation
  • Wood-fired sauna with rosemary from the For Rest gardens
  • Aromatic essential oils and electrolyte drinks
  • Ice plunges and guided recovery
  • Deep stress release and nervous system reset
Thursday Afternoon

The Full Experience

By Thursday you know the rhythm. This session goes deeper -- longer rounds, more intense contrast, and a group that has been through a week together.

  • Full hot and cold cycle protocol
  • Advanced breathing and cold exposure
  • Guided sauna rounds with scent and sound
  • Ice plunges and active recovery
  • Integration and reflection
  • Followed by moonlight yoga at 19:00
The Week

Day by Day

Enough structure to switch off, enough freedom to make it your own.

Day 1Sunday

Arrive from 15:00. Settle in, explore. Welcome Cyprus BBQ dinner at 19:00 -- sheftalies, souvlaki, Greek salad around the table. Hot tub relaxation under the stars at 21:00.

Day 2Monday

Morning green smoothies and village tour and hilltop hike. Community brunch back at the glamping. Free time from 14:00. 9 Waves sauna and ice therapy session at 16:00 (2-3 hours). Dinner at the local village tavern.

Day 3Tuesday

Morning yoga and detox bowl brunch. Shuttle to the beach at 12:00 -- swim, kayak, or relax by the Mediterranean. Back at 16:00. Community cooking dinner -- hands on, sleeves rolled up. Board games, hot tub, and stars.

Day 4Wednesday

Barefoot breakfast picnic at Governor's Beach at 08:00. Free day to explore Cyprus -- beach, cycling, horse riding, massage, or reading in a hammock. Traditional Cyprus meze dinner at a local tavern in the evening.

Day 5Thursday

Community breakfast. Beach shuttle at 11:30. Back at 15:00. 9 Waves sauna and ice session at 16:00. Moonlight yoga at 19:00. Light community dinner under the stars.

Day 6Friday

Farewell breakfast together -- sharing reflections and new friendships. Check-out by 11:00. Take your final deep breath of fresh Cyprus air and head home rested and recharged.

The Setting

A Hidden Valley in the Cyprus Hills

For Rest is tucked into a hidden valley, perched on a breezy ridge, with goat tracks leading up to a stunning viewpoint and clearings that look out over the quaint village of Agios Theodoros.

The passion project of hosts Loucas and Polina -- every inch of the unique glamping space has been lovingly dreamt into life. Private tents, quiet mornings, unhurried meals, and time that belongs only to you.

Questions

Good to Know

Do I really have to hand in my phone?

Yes -- that's the idea. It'll be safely stored in a secure lockbox. If being completely offline feels daunting, there are two optional phone access windows during the week. Before arrival you'll receive an emergency contact number for your family.

I've never used a sauna or ice bath. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Paul will explain everything before your first session and guide you throughout. No experience needed -- the sessions are suitable for complete beginners.

Are solo travellers welcome?

Completely. Some come alone to be alone. Others find friendships they didn't expect. Still others think they want to isolate and find themselves opening up more than ever. All of it is perfect.

Is it physically demanding?

Not especially. Hikes include some hills but are short and taken at a relaxed pace. The sauna and ice sessions are optional and beginner-friendly. If you want to push yourself, that can also be arranged.

Do I have to take part in everything?

Not at all. Everything is included in your price, but nothing is compulsory. If you'd rather spend an afternoon in a hammock or walking on your own, that's completely fine. This is your week.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, swimwear, walking shoes, a light jumper for evenings, a reusable water bottle, and a book you've been meaning to read. A notebook or journal is optional but often becomes a retreat highlight.

What's the cancellation policy?

100% refund on your deposit if you cancel 1 or more days before the retreat start date. The remaining balance is due upon arrival. We accept credit/debit card or bank transfer.

Is there WiFi?

Yes -- but don't worry, you won't need it. In case of a genuine emergency, the WiFi works well and will cover you if you need to get online.

Is a shorter or longer stay possible?

Reach out to us and let us know what you're thinking and we'll see what we can do!

Do you cater to dietary requirements?

Yes -- vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, keto, nut-allergy-friendly and more. Just let us know when booking.
