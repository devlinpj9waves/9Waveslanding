The Idea

Six days to slow down,
move your body and reconnect.

Six days to step away from your usual routine, spend more time outside, move your body and enjoy a slower way of living.

Wake up in nature. Stay in comfortable glamping tents surrounded by hills and old olive trees. Start your mornings outside, enjoy slow evenings under the stars and have time to simply do nothing.

Hike. Move. Swim. Sauna. Cold plunge. Share the experience with a small international group of maximum 15 guests — and still have time for yourself.

Join the activities, take a book to your tent, enjoy a slow morning or simply relax and take in the views.

The 9 Waves Experience

Fire & Ice in the Cyprus Hills

Two guided contrast therapy sessions led by Paul. A physical reset that matches the pace of the week.

Monday Afternoon

Introduction to Hot & Cold Therapy

Your first session eases you in with the science and practice of contrast therapy. By the end you'll wonder why you ever needed a phone to feel alive.

  • Introduction to the benefits of hot and cold therapy
  • Breathing techniques and preparation
  • Wood-fired sauna with rosemary from the For Rest gardens
  • Aromatic essential oils and electrolyte drinks
  • Ice plunges and guided recovery
  • Deep stress release and nervous system reset
Thursday Afternoon

The Full Experience

By Thursday you know the rhythm. This session goes deeper -- longer rounds, more intense contrast, and a group that has been through a week together.

  • Full hot and cold cycle protocol
  • Advanced breathing and cold exposure
  • Guided sauna rounds with scent and sound
  • Ice plunges and active recovery
  • Integration and reflection
  • Followed by moonlight yoga at 19:00
The Week

Day by Day

Enough structure to switch off, enough freedom to make it your own.

Day 1Sunday

15:00 – Arrival & Check-in

Settle into your glamping tent, unpack, make yourself at home, and take your first slow breath surrounded by the peaceful Cyprus countryside. Your reset starts here.

19:00 – Welcome Cyprus BBQ Dinner

We begin our week the Cypriot way — gathered around the table, sharing traditional local food, stories, and the first moments together. Enjoy a traditional Cyprus BBQ with sheftalies, chicken souvlaki, boiled baby potatoes, Greek salad, warm Greek pitas, and fresh seasonal fruit from the village gardens. This is our welcome evening — a chance to meet the people you'll be sharing the next six days with, hear each other's stories, and set the intention for a beautiful week together.

21:00 – Hot Tub Relaxation Under the Stars

Time to let go of the stress and tension you arrived with. Unwind in the warm outdoor hot tub, listen to the sounds of nature, take in the rolling hills, and enjoy your first peaceful evening beneath the Cyprus night sky.

Day 2Monday

08:30 – Morning Fuel: Smoothies & Protein Shakes

Start the day light but nourished with fresh green smoothies and protein shakes, packed with nutrients and natural energy to fuel your morning hike.

09:00 – Village Tour & Hilltop Hike (3–3.5 hours)

A slow walk through the traditional Agios Theodoros village, quiet streets, local stories, hidden corners, and an authentic Cypriot atmosphere, followed by a scenic hike to the highest hilltop of the village with breathtaking panoramic views over the Cyprus countryside.

11:30 – Panoramic Hilltop Brunch

Brunch tastes better with a view. Settle in at the top of the hill and enjoy a relaxed picnic-style brunch surrounded by nature and panoramic countryside views. Freshly baked croissants with ham/turkey & cheese, boiled eggs, seasonal fruit and fresh vegetables — a nourishing recharge after the hike.

13:00–16:00 – Free Time at the Glamping

Relax in a hammock, read a book, journal, enjoy your hot tub, take a nap, or simply slow down and reconnect with yourself.

16:00 – Hot & Cold Therapy Experience with Paul (2–3 hours)

Introduction to the benefits of hot and cold therapy, breathing techniques, guided sauna rituals with aromatic essential oils and rosemary infusions, ice plunges, recovery practices, and deep stress release.

19:00 – Community Dinner

Come together around the table for another relaxed evening of traditional Cyprus food, good conversation, and time with the community. Traditional Cyprus pita souvlaki (chicken or pork, and/or sheftalies), potatoes, Greek salad, tahini, and homemade keftedes.

20:00 – Evening Wind-Down

Hot tub relaxation, board games, hammock reading, meaningful conversations, and peaceful outdoor glamping vibes under the stars.

Day 3Tuesday

08:30 – Morning Yoga

Start your day with gentle yoga and movement on the edge of the hill, overlooking the traditional Cyprus village below. Fresh morning air, birds singing, rolling hills all around.

09:30 – Healthy Community Breakfast

A nourishing, protein-rich bowl of Greek yogurt, muesli, oats, honey, crushed almonds, and protein — fresh, balanced, and full of energy for the day ahead.

11:30 – Shuttle to the Mediterranean Coast & Free Time

Enjoy some free time by the Mediterranean Sea. Relax on a sunbed by the water, grab a fresh drink, or visit a local seaside tavern for fresh seafood and the catch of the day. For those who feel like exploring, take a scenic coastal hike along the beautiful white rocks, with open views over the Mediterranean.

16:00 – Shuttle Back to the Glamping

18:00 – Community Cooking Dinner

Relax, roll up your sleeves, and gently prepare a traditional Cyprus-inspired dinner together — fresh meat and fish, seasonal salads, traditional Cyprus pitas, and a selection of local dips such as tahini, taramasalata, tzatziki, and hummus.

20:00 – Slow Evening

Hot tub relaxation, board games, hammock reading, meaningful conversations, and peaceful outdoor glamping vibes under the stars.

Day 4Wednesday

08:00–08:30 – Shuttle to Governor's Beach & White Stones

Start the morning with a short ride to the Mediterranean coast and the beautiful White Stones of Governor's Beach.

08:30–09:00 – Outdoor Circuit Training

Wake up your body with a 20–30 minute outdoor circuit training session by the sea — light, energising movement designed to get your blood flowing.

09:00 – Barefoot Picnic by the Sea

Take off your shoes, feel the grass beneath your feet, and slow down. A mindful barefoot picnic designed to help you feel grounded and reconnect with the land, the sea, and the present moment. A fresh, revitalising breakfast basket surrounded by nature, with the Mediterranean just beside you.

10:00–19:00 – Free Day to Explore Cyprus

Spend the day however you like: relax back at glamping, visit nearby Limassol Marina or Larnaca city, book a massage session (pre-booking required), or continue your slow retreat with reading, journaling, or relaxing in nature.

19:00 – Traditional Cyprus Meze Dinner

Community dinner at a traditional Cyprus tavern featuring authentic local meze. Come back to the village by this time, or feel free to have dinner in the city and return at your own pace.

20:00 – Slow Evening

Hot tub relaxation, board games, hammock reading, meaningful conversations, and peaceful outdoor glamping vibes under the stars.

Day 5Thursday

08:30 – Outdoor Circuit Training

Wake up your body with a 20–30 minute outdoor circuit training session.

09:00 – Community Cyprus-Style Breakfast

By now, you're already becoming a professional in Cyprus food — so this morning, we cook breakfast together! Traditional Cyprus breakfast with fresh bread, grilled halloumi, lountza, pastourma, eggs, tomatoes, cucumber, warm pitas and local olives. Good food, slow morning, and everyone around the table.

11:30 – Shuttle to the Beach

Free time at local beach bars. Swim in the crystal-clear Mediterranean Sea, do watersports, enjoy lunch, refreshing drinks, or simply relax by the sea.

15:00 – Shuttle Back to the Glamping

16:00 – Hot & Cold Therapy Experience with Paul: Wood-Fired Sauna & Ice Plunge

Step into the heat of a wood-burning sauna, filled with the scent of rosemary. Sweat, breathe, slow down — then step outside and plunge into the ice-cold water. Feel your whole body wake up: alive, clear and energised. And afterwards, sink into the hot tub and relax.

18:30 – Moonlight Yoga & Movement

As the sun disappears, we slow everything down. Only the hills, the stars, the moonlight and you. A gentle evening yoga & movement session surrounded by the warm glow of fire, with the Cyprus countryside disappearing into darkness around you.

19:30 – Community Dinner

A relaxed dinner together, prepared with fresh, local Cypriot ingredients — seasonal vegetables, meat and other local products.

20:30 – Slow Evening

Hot tub relaxation, board games, hammock reading, meaningful conversations, and peaceful outdoor glamping vibes under the stars.

Day 6Friday

08:30 – Farewell Breakfast

One more breakfast together around the table — good food, fresh Cyprus air, favourite moments from the week, and people who were strangers only six days ago. A morning to laugh, share memories, exchange contacts, make plans to meet again, and enjoy a little more time together before heading home.

Before 12:00 – Check-out & See You Again

Take one more look at the hills, breathe in the fresh air, hug your new friends — and take a little piece of Cyprus with you. You came for a reset. Leave recharged, energised, connected, and ready to bring that feeling back into everyday life.

The Setting

A Hidden Valley in the Cyprus Hills

For Rest is tucked into a hidden valley, perched on a breezy ridge, with goat tracks leading up to a stunning viewpoint and clearings that look out over the quaint village of Agios Theodoros.

The passion project of hosts Loucas and Polina -- every inch of the unique glamping space has been lovingly dreamt into life. Private tents, quiet mornings, unhurried meals, and time that belongs only to you.

Location Agios Theodoros, Larnaca District, Cyprus
Airport Larnaca (LCA) -- pickup €30 one-way, €50 return. Paphos (PFO) -- contact us
Weather 20–24°C days, 12–17°C evenings. Mediterranean still warm enough for a swim. November brings the hills turning green, migratory birds and pink flamingos at the salt lakes
By car Along the coast or motorway. Parking on site. A 4×4 is helpful but not required

Questions

Good to Know

Can I hand in my phone?

You're welcome to — many guests do and love it. We'll store it safely and you can collect it any time. It's entirely your choice. Before arrival, you'll receive an emergency contact number your family can use to reach you during the week.

I've never used a sauna or ice bath. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Paul will explain everything before your first session and guide you throughout. No experience needed -- the sessions are suitable for complete beginners.

Are solo travellers welcome?

Completely. Some come alone to be alone. Others find friendships they didn't expect. Still others think they want to isolate and find themselves opening up more than ever. All of it is perfect.

Is it physically demanding?

Not especially. Hikes include some hills but are short and taken at a relaxed pace. The sauna and ice sessions are optional and beginner-friendly. If you want to push yourself, that can also be arranged.

Do I have to take part in everything?

Not at all. Everything is included in your price, but nothing is compulsory. If you'd rather spend an afternoon in a hammock or walking on your own, that's completely fine. This is your week.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, swimwear, walking shoes, a light jumper for evenings, a reusable water bottle, and a book you've been meaning to read. A notebook or journal is optional but often becomes a retreat highlight.

What's the cancellation policy?

100% refund on your deposit if you cancel 1 or more days before the retreat start date. The remaining balance is due upon arrival. We accept credit/debit card or bank transfer.

Is there WiFi?

Yes -- but don't worry, you won't need it. In case of a genuine emergency, the WiFi works well and will cover you if you need to get online.

Is a shorter or longer stay possible?

Reach out to us and let us know what you're thinking and we'll see what we can do!

Do you cater to dietary requirements?

Yes -- vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, keto, nut-allergy-friendly and more. Just let us know when booking.
