Questions

Good to Know

What can I expect from the experience?
This experience is an invitation to slow down, reconnect with your body, and create space for rest and renewal. Throughout the session you'll enjoy a combination of sauna, swimming, yoga, gentle lymphatic drainage movements, and an optional guided fast supported by a nourishing drink.
I've never used a sauna or ice bath. Is that okay?
Absolutely. Paul will explain everything before your first session and guide you throughout. No experience needed — the sessions are suitable for complete beginners.
Is sauna use mandatory?
Not at all. Every part of the experience is an invitation, not an obligation. You can choose what feels right for your body, take breaks whenever needed, and skip any activity you're not comfortable with.
Do I need to participate in every activity?
Not at all. You are encouraged to listen to your body and move at your own pace. Feel free to rest, take a break, or adapt any activity to suit your needs. There is no pressure to do everything.
What should I bring with me?
We recommend bringing 2 water bottles, 2 towels, 1 yoga mat and cover, swimwear, comfortable clothing for yoga and movement, and any personal essentials. If you choose not to fast, please bring a light snack.
Are there any health considerations I should be aware of?
Your well-being comes first. Sauna use and fasting may not be suitable for everyone, particularly during pregnancy or for people with certain medical conditions or medications. Please consult your healthcare professional if you have any concerns, and let us know privately about any needs we should be aware of. You are always welcome to skip an activity.
How can I prepare for the experience?
We recommend arriving well-rested and hydrated, wearing comfortable clothing, and coming with an open mind. Please avoid arriving dehydrated or pushing yourself physically before the session. If you choose to participate in the guided fast, please avoid eating solid food for at least 2 hours before the session.
